Bills found for you

Repeating charges and paychecks are detected from history. A weekly grocery run is treated as a habit, not a bill, and subscriptions you no longer use get flagged before they renew.

Privacy

Your data stays yours

The short version. The privacy policy has the detail.

No ads, no tracking, no sale

The app has no advertising and no analytics. Your data is not sold or shared for marketing. This site sets no cookies and loads nothing from other companies.

Bank access stays on the server

The app never sees your bank password. The key that reads your bank activity is encrypted on the server and is never stored on a phone.

Take it or delete it

Export everything your household holds as one file. Delete your account from Settings, and the data goes with it.
